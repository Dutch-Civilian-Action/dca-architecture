# DCA Ukraine Operations — Semantic Structure

## Purpose
This Shared Drive holds DCA's operational mission and field reality connected to Ukraine.

## What belongs here
- mission planning and mission packages;
- execution and field records;
- operational partner communication;
- verification media and delivery/handover evidence;
- receipts and reports generated from missions or field activity;
- field notes;
- story intake captured from operational reality before communication production.

## What does not belong here
- fundraising campaign management;
- final marketing/storytelling production merely because the source came from Ukraine;
- technical CRM/Airtable schemas or automation implementation;
- organisation-wide conclusions derived from many missions once they have been consolidated into shared organisational reality.

## Hierarchy semantics
This drive can follow the operational chain:
planning → mission/execution → partner coordination → verification/evidence → reporting/receipts → field notes → story intake.

Verification evidence and storytelling media are not the same thing. Operational verification remains here; communication production belongs in Marketing & Storytelling and should reference the source evidence where required.

## Machine routing rule
Route here when the artifact is produced by or directly evidences Ukraine mission/field operations. Derived organisation-wide truth belongs in Organisation; technical implementation belongs in Systems & Data.

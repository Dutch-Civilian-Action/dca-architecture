# DCA Warehouse & Logistics — Semantic Structure

## Purpose
This Shared Drive is the authoritative operational space for DCA's warehouse and logistics work.

## What belongs here
- current warehouse/logistics procedures and standards;
- operational planning and working material;
- goods intake, handling, sorting, packing, shipment preparation, and transport-related operational records;
- logistics-specific partner and volunteer material;
- physical labels and other operational materials;
- logistics inputs requiring validation;
- bounded logistics pilots and their evidence;
- historical logistics material in archive.

## What does not belong here
- Airtable schemas, field specifications, automations, or technical integrations;
- organisation-wide structure or operating-model definitions;
- fundraising campaign material even when it funds a transport;
- finance reconciliation even when it concerns logistics costs.

## Hierarchy semantics
Use lifecycle zones where useful:
- authoritative standards / current procedures;
- planning / working;
- inputs / validation;
- pilots / tests;
- operational records / materials;
- archive.

A pilot stays a pilot until DCA deliberately adopts the validated practice as current procedure.

## Machine routing rule
Route here when the artifact is part of performing or evidencing warehouse/logistics work. Route the technical implementation of that work to Systems & Data.

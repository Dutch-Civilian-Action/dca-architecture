# DCA Marketing & Storytelling — Semantic Structure

## Purpose
This Shared Drive holds DCA's brand, communication strategy, storytelling production, and published communication outputs.

## What belongs here
- brand guidelines and brand assets;
- marketing/communication strategy;
- raw media gathered for communication production;
- content-production working files;
- published content;
- newsletter material;
- website content and copy;
- print and event communication material;
- communication archive and working/validation areas when needed.

## What does not belong here
- operational reality as a substitute for source evidence;
- mission verification evidence whose primary purpose is proving delivery or handover;
- fundraising outreach execution;
- technical website/data integrations or system architecture.

## Source-of-truth rule
Marketing and copy should be produced from DCA's shared operational reality and the relevant functional evidence. Marketing does not become the source of truth about what DCA operationally did.

Operational reality → shared source → functional communication judgment → content/output.

## Media boundary
- verification/evidence media → operational drive, especially Ukraine Operations;
- communication-production media → Marketing & Storytelling;
- reference across drives rather than duplicating uncontrolled sources.

## Machine routing rule
Route here when the artifact's primary purpose is brand, communication, storytelling, content production, or publication. Route operational evidence to its functional source and technical implementation to Systems & Data.

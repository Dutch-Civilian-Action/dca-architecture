# DCA Organisation — Semantic Structure

## Purpose
This Shared Drive holds what DCA knows about itself at organisation level and how DCA is organised.

It is the canonical home for two distinct things that must remain distinguishable:

1. **Shared organisational reality** — what is true, known, reported, validated, uncertain, or historically evidenced across DCA.
2. **Structure and governance** — how DCA organises functions, roles, responsibilities, boundaries, decision rights, policies, and organisation-wide ways of working.

## What belongs here
- current shared organisational reality and derived organisational reality;
- validation/evidence used to establish organisation-wide reality;
- historical organisational-reality reconstructions and source evidence;
- current organisational structure and governance material;
- working structure/governance material that is not yet authoritative;
- superseded organisational-structure material preserved in archive;
- organisation-wide policies and official organisational material;
- people/onboarding knowledge that is organisation-wide;
- organisation-wide meeting/decision records;
- handovers and organisational knowledge that do not belong to one functional operation.

## What does not belong here
- function-specific operational procedures or records that belong to Logistics, Fundraising, Finance, Ukraine Operations, or Marketing & Storytelling;
- Airtable schemas, technical integrations, automations, AI configuration, or system implementation details;
- formal Board records whose authority derives from Board action.

## Hierarchy semantics
The important distinction is not the exact folder names but the meaning:

### Shared organisational reality
- current canonical reality;
- validation / evidence awaiting consolidation;
- historical reality / reconstruction archive.

### Structure and governance
- current structure / governance;
- working structure / governance;
- superseded structure / governance archive.

### Organisation-wide knowledge
- policies / official material;
- people / onboarding;
- meetings / decisions;
- handovers / organisational knowledge.

## Boundary rules
- Shared organisational truth belongs here after evidence has been reconciled across functions.
- Function-specific source evidence remains with the function that generated or owns it.
- Organisational structure belongs here even when represented in Airtable or another platform.
- The technical schema representing organisational structure belongs in Systems & Data.
- Formal Board approvals/minutes belong in DCA Board; reference the canonical organisational artifact rather than duplicating it.

## Machine routing rule
Route here when the artifact describes organisation-wide reality, organisational structure/governance, or organisation-wide knowledge. Do not route function-specific operations or technical implementation here merely because they affect the whole organisation.
